What types of writing do you do (e.g. fiction, nonfiction, poetry)?
>I predominantly work with poetry and prose-poetry. I also write in spaces of critical theory.

What tools (or techniques) do you use to write? Why do you use them?
> I will at times use time constraints. I find that the rapid periods of writing without the ability to actively contemplate exactly what I'm writing provides interesting results.

How do these tools shape the way you write (e.g., constrain or generate)?
> They are quite generative. They allow for ideas I had been unable to articulate to emerge in unexpected places with something resembling definition.


What are you curious about writing in the digital environment?
> I'm interested in moving beyond linearity. I'm not overly concerned with content in the digital space at this point, but more interested in the dimensions it will open.
