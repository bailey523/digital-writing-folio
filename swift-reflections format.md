## Week 2 — Wednesday
*200–400 words | Assessment 1 Process Documentation*


**Option A — Hayles (2007):** What are some of the constraints and tensions that Hayles notes have motivated writers to experiment in the field of E-Lit?

Hayles identifies several productive tensions and constraints that have pushed electronic literature writers toward experimentation. She highlights the tension between narrative control and user agency. Giving audiences access to raw creative materials risks undermining the narrative experience, while withholding control, frustrates the promise of interactivity. Writers have had to devise structural solutions to overcome this. these can be observed in Storyspace's guard fields that gate access to certain lexia and Aristotelian constraints built into Façade, where they help to maintain dramatic shape while still accommodating user input. The medium's technical nature has also been a creative pressure. Electronic text cannot be accessed without being performed by code, which means writers must grapple with the collaboration between human imagination and machine logic. This is visible in generative and randomizing works, where the algorithm becomes a co-author of sorts.
